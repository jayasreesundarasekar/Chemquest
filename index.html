THEORY & LEARNING

Understanding SMILES

Atoms are represented by element symbols; parentheses indicate branches, digits mark ring closures, and bond symbols describe connectivity. Lowercase symbols represent aromatic atoms.

Descriptors summarize structural features. They are computational estimates and do not replace experimental measurements.

STRUCTURE–PROPERTY ANALYSIS

What do descriptors tell us?

LogP: estimated lipophilicity. TPSA: topological polar surface area. HBD/HBA: hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors. Rotatable bonds: a simple flexibility indicator.

Analyze a molecule to see its descriptor profile.
